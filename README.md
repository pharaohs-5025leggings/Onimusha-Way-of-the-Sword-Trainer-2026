## 🔥 Project Overview

**This tool** is a comprehensive trainer for **Onimusha: Way of the Sword**. This trainer provides optional gameplay assistance for Onimusha: Way of the Sword. It supports Musashi's core systems, including health, Oni power, soul absorption, combat endurance, and movement speed, while leaving the game's swordplay, parries, Issen counters, Oni Armaments, and exploration intact.

**Target Audience:** Players who want a more accessible single-player experience, practice Issen timing and parries, explore Kyoto without repeated deaths, test Oni Armaments, or reduce grinding while preserving the game's combat and story.

> **⚠️ IMPORTANT:** This tool is for **educational and private use only**. It does not modify any game files — all changes are temporary and reset when the game closes. The tool is **undetectable**, but **use it at your own risk**. Always back up your save files before use.

## 📖 Detailed Feature Guide

### Movement & Exploration

Move through the game's third-person environments normally and use the trainer's movement options only when needed. A speed modifier can make traversal and backtracking quicker, while normal controls remain best for precise positioning during parries, dodges, and boss encounters.

### Tools & Inventory

Keep the game's normal equipment and item management available. Healing items, Oni Armaments, charms, and other resources should be selected through the game's menus. Unlimited consumables and unlimited souls can reduce resource pressure, but they do not automatically equip items or replace the game's upgrade systems.

### World & Environment

Way of the Sword combines a mostly linear story with detailed Edo-period locations, combat arenas, exploration sections, and optional activities. Use invincibility or unlimited health while learning unfamiliar routes, investigating side areas, and collecting souls. Save normally at in-game save points and avoid changing values during cinematics or loading screens.

### Combat & Weapons

Combat centers on sword strikes, parries, deflects, dodges, Issen counters, enemy stamina, soul absorption, and Oni Armaments. For training, enable Easy Issen Timing and Unlimited Stamina. For a faster playthrough, combine Damage Multiplier with One-Hit Break. Infinite Health and Oni Power are useful for learning boss patterns, while Unlimited Souls supports repeated use of the Oni Gauntlet and special attacks.
